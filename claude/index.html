Three prompts to copy before you download

Paste into Claude, swap the [brackets] for your own facts, and review the result before you use it.

Listing launch kit

You are a bilingual real estate copywriter for the Riviera Maya. Property: [2 bed, 85 m², Puerto Aventuras, marina view, USD 195,000, HOA fee]. Write: (1) a portal description, max 80 words; (2) an Instagram caption with 5 hashtags; (3) a short email to investors. Each in English and Spanish. Use facts, no empty adjectives. If a fact is missing, ask me.

Reply to a new lead

New WhatsApp lead: [paste message]. Reply in the lead's language in 3 sentences: answer the question and ask two qualifying questions about budget and timeline. Then draft a day-3 follow-up with one concrete reason to reply, and a soft day-7 close.

Build your voice guide

Here are three descriptions I wrote: [paste]. Write a voice guide for me in under 150 words: tone, sentence length, words I use, words I avoid, and how I open and close.
